Opening a separate search result, clicking a paid advertisement, using another cashback extension, or switching from a desktop browser to the Target app can interfere with attribution.

ShopBack states that rejected, canceled, refunded, exchanged, or returned purchases are not eligible for cashback. Its support documentation also notes that returns and cancellations can reverse cashback.

Wait until the order is final before treating the cashback as part of your savings.

ShopBack says Target needs to confirm the transaction through cookies. Accepting the store’s cookies during the shopping session can help maintain tracking. Privacy tools and browser settings that block required cookies may prevent the purchase from being recorded.
